About these counts

These are visits to the homepage (/ and /index.html), not to the news, events, or visits pages. A visit is not a unique person; one person can visit more than once.

Counts come from Cloudflare Web Analytics. Traffic classified as bots is excluded, but this does not identify every bot. Ad blockers, disabled JavaScript, and other recording failures can leave visits uncounted.

Archived counts may be estimates. Only completed calendar days in Europe/Berlin are included; missing days are left as gaps.
